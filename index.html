        How to play

        Goal

        Be the first to empty your hand. You score the points left in every rival's hand.

        Matching

        Play a card that matches the top discard by suit or rank.

        Eights are wild

        Play an 8 on anything, then declare the suit the next player must match. Eights left in hand cost 50 points.

        Drawing

        If you can't play, draw from the stock — up to 3 cards, then your turn passes.

        Controls

        • ←/→ choose a card, Enter play it
        • D draw · H hint · U undo (practice) · Esc pause
        • Tap a highlighted card to play it; tap Draw to take cards.

        Fair play

        Every deal comes from a visible seed. Identical seeds and moves replay identically.
